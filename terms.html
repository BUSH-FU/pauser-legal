一、关于本应用1. About the App

Pauser 是一款 iOS 上的个人防沉迷工具,核心功能是: Pauser is a personal screen-time tool for iOS. Its core purpose is to:

  • 让用户选择想要被守护的应用(例如抖音、小红书、Instagram、YouTube Shorts 等)let you pick which apps you want to guard (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Reddit, or anything that grabs you);
